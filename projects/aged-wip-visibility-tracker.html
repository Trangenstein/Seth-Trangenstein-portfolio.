The problem

Work-in-process had no standing visibility once it entered the system. A job could sit well past its normal cycle time — waiting on a part, a routing step, or a floor packet discrepancy — and nobody would know until it showed up as a missed ship date. Aging WIP was invisible until it was already a crisis, and by the time it surfaced in a leadership review, the options for fixing it were already narrow.

What I built

An Excel workbook that ingests the daily SAP WIP export, calculates each job's age against its expected cycle time, and automatically flags anything crossing defined thresholds — Watch, Aging, or Critical — using INDEX/MATCH and SUMIFS formulas rather than manual sorting.

A summary view rolls the part-level detail up into a format leadership could scan in minutes: aging WIP broken out by work center, with the oldest and highest-risk jobs surfaced first instead of buried in a flat export.

  • Daily ingestion of the SAP WIP export with no manual re-formatting required
  • Automatic age calculation against each part's expected cycle time
  • Three-tier flagging (Watch / Aging / Critical) built on INDEX/MATCH and SUMIFS
  • Weekly leadership rollup summarizing exposure by part and work center

Impact

Gave leadership a standing, self-updating view of aging WIP by part and work center — turning a reactive scramble into a proactive conversation about where to expedite, reallocate capacity, or escalate a supply issue before it hit the schedule. Aging WIP stopped being something the team discovered and started being something the team managed.
